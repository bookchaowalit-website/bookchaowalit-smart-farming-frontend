# Smart Farming — Seasonal Fieldbook

## Visual world

A field notebook translated into a dark green reading room: soil is the ground, straw is the active marker, and every crop is a bed that can be visited in sequence.

## System

- Palette: soil `#263329`, linen `#eee8d8`, leaf `#718650`, sprout `#b4c77d`, straw `#e1c45a`, wet-edge blue `#8eafb0`.
- Type: a quiet humanist sans for crop names and Courier New for field coordinates.
- Composition: crop shelf → current field reading → six-week visit rail.
- Signature interaction: changing the crop changes the bed note, facts, diagram, and task sequence; the week rail changes the next visit.
- Responsive rule: the shelf becomes a vertical seed list and the week rail becomes a 3×2 field grid.

## Integrity

All crop guidance is illustrative. Sensor, weather, marketplace, and agronomy claims stay explicitly outside the surface.
