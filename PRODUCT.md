# Smart Farming — Product Truth

Smart Farming is a seasonal fieldbook for planning crop cycles, water needs, and field observations. It is a synthetic knowledge demo, not agronomic advice or a connected farm sensor system.

## User and job

- Audience: a small grower planning a manageable mixed crop bed.
- Primary job: choose a crop, understand its seasonal window, and turn the window into a simple weekly field plan.
- Mode: read and operate.

## Boundaries

- Crop timings, water amounts, and observations are illustrative examples.
- No weather feed, soil sensor, marketplace, or regional agronomy claim is made.
- The first release is a focused browser fieldbook with local interaction.
